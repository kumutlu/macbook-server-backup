Privacy

MacBook Server Backup is a private tool used by a single person to back up that person's own machine to that person's own Google Drive account. It has no other users.

Data it handles

Snapshots are encrypted on the owner's own machine before anything is uploaded. Only the encrypted result is stored, and only in the owner's own Google Drive. No data is sent anywhere else.

What it can access

The tool uses the Google permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which limits it to the files and folders it created itself. It cannot reach any other content in the account.

Third parties

There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking, and no data is sold or shared with anyone.

Retention and removal

Deleting the backup folder in Google Drive, or revoking the app's access from the Google Account permissions page, removes the stored backup data.
